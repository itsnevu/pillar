Instant Sub-Second Settlement vs The Five-Day Wire

If you send a five thousand dollar wire transfer from London to San Francisco on a Friday afternoon, your capital immediately vanishes into the black box of correspondent banking. For the next five days, neither you nor the recipient knows exactly where the funds reside. The money navigates through intermediary clearing houses, incurs foreign exchange markups, and triggers random compliance holds before trickling into the contractor's bank account on Wednesday morning minus fifty dollars in transfer fees.

This banking latency is not an immutable law of physics; it is the artifact of forty-year-old batch-processing database architectures.

On Arc Chain, payment settlement is cryptographic and instantaneous. When a client clicks "Release Funds" on Pyris Pact, consensus is achieved and state is finalized in under one second. The contractor receives spendable USDC directly in their non-custodial wallet with absolute finality.

Zero intermediary deductions, zero correspondent banking delays, and zero black-box uncertainty. For global teams building the future, programmable money is the only infrastructure that makes sense.
