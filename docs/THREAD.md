Clients fear paying upfront retainers to unverified contractors who might ghost.

Contractors fear delivering completed code without verified funds in place.

3/

In exchange for holding money in a database, centralized networks take a 10%–20% cut of your hard work, enforce arbitrary holds, and lock accounts.

It's 2026. Code can do this better.

Enter Pyris Pact: programmable milestone escrow built on Arc Chain.

A client deposits USDC into smart contract escrow.
The contractor sees funds locked onchain.
Deliverables are submitted.
Client approves with one click.
USDC disburses instantly.

5/
Why Arc Chain?

Because on Arc Chain, native gas is paid directly in USDC.

Zero ETH needed. Zero volatile token friction for enterprise accounting teams.

A $5,000 milestone costs $0.002 in gas, paid in the exact same USDC.

If scope changes, the contractor can voluntarily cancel and refund the client at any time.
