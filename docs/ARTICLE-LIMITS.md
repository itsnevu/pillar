What Trustless Milestone Escrow Can and Cannot Do

Any financial protocol that refuses to state its limits with total candor is selling marketing brochures rather than shipping enterprise software. Smart contracts are extraordinarily good at executing deterministic state machines, and they are completely incapable of judging subjective human aesthetics.

Here is what Pyris Pact does with mathematical certainty: it holds deposited USDC in non-custodial custody, guarantees that neither the developers nor unauthorized actors can siphon funds, enforces hard deadline constraints, and guarantees instant sub-second disbursal the instant the client authorizes payment.

Here is what smart contracts cannot do: an EVM opcode cannot determine whether a brand design is visually stunning or whether a Next.js frontend is sufficiently responsive. Qualitative review will always remain an offchain human evaluation.

Understanding this boundary is why Pyris Pact is designed around explicit milestone submissions, client verification, and dispute state flagging. By automating the payment rails while preserving human review of the deliverable, we achieve security without pretending code can evaluate art.
